# PSC2 landing page: conversion wireframe

**Business:** Pacific Softcom Inc., Long Beach, CA. Certified 3CX partner with 35 years in the telephone business. Installs IP phone systems, SIP trunks, hotel PMS phone integration and remote-office phones. Sells phones that arrive set up for the customer's system.

**Audience:** owners, office managers and IT leads at businesses with roughly 5 to 100 phones, especially multi-site companies and hotels, who pay a phone company or hosted provider too much and dread a messy switch.

**Page job (primary conversion):** send in the current phone bill for a free review (form).
**Secondary conversion:** call 562-489-7760, or leave a number for a call back.

**Proof we can use truthfully:** three documented installs (Pacific Sailing & Motor Yachts, The Padre Hotel, BatchMaster), published hourly support rates, 35 years in telephony, the 3CX partner status. No invented testimonials, staff, ratings or press logos.

**Layout source:** Youlink broadband template, section for section. The hero is a straight copy of the Youlink hero (3-slide fade slider, subtitle with underline, 80px title with amber squiggle, price + bonus row, pill button, swinging shards, dotted globe, drifting dot, long arrows on the right). Colors move from Youlink blue to PSC teal; the Youlink amber stays as the accent because PSC's own site has always used yellow on blue.

Visitor journey the order follows: **what is it → is it for me → can I trust them → what does it cost → how hard is the switch → what about my worries → act.**
