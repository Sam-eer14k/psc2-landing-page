SIP trunking

A SIP trunk is an outside line that comes in over your internet connection. You stop renting copper lines from the phone company, and calls between your own offices never touch their network.

For: anyone paying the phone company by the line.

  • Calls go out over the internet
  • Offices dial each other on three-digit extensions
  • A second internet line just for voice if calls drop

Hardware advice from our installs

Short notes on gear questions we get asked a lot.

Networking2 min read

Why a PoE switch pays for itself

Read the note

A Power over Ethernet switch powers each phone through its network cable. Put one battery backup next to the switch and every phone stays up when the power goes out. The same switch runs paging speakers, door phones, access points and cameras without extra outlets. Phones need their own power supplies otherwise, so PoE often costs less. And because the switch is managed, we can restart every phone after an upgrade without visiting each desk.

Paging1 min read

One IP paging horn vs. a whole analog setup

Read the note

IP paging horns aren't cheap. But for one horn in a warehouse, or one per zone across a few zones, a 5-watt IP horn still costs less than an analog gateway, a page adapter, an amplifier and horns. It's tidier too. If you already have a legacy paging system, or need several 70-watt horns in one spot, call us and we'll connect to what you have.

Phones1 min read

Why "cheap" phones online end up costing more

Read the note

Plug in an IP phone straight from an online store and it usually won't register. Someone has to update the firmware and enter your system's settings, and technicians bill $80 to $150 an hour for that. Our phones arrive upgraded and set up for your system, with a guide to the features you actually have. If one doesn't work when you plug it in, we make it work at no charge.
